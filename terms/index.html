Plans change. This policy applies to every session booked through this site. If a specific service needs different terms, they will be stated on that service's page or in your confirmation. Time reserved for a session is time I can't offer anyone else, so the amount of notice determines what happens:

Reschedules are subject to availability; I'll offer the earliest open date that works for both of us.
