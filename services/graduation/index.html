Before Your Session

Sessions can take place before or after your ceremony, on or near campus, or at another location around Charlotte. Mini sessions focus on classic cap and gown portraits, while Standard sessions allow for a second outfit or a different look.

During Your Session

No posing experience needed. I'll guide you through a mix of classic portraits, natural movement, and campus landmarks. Family and friends are welcome to join for a few photos. Group bookings include individual portraits of each graduate as well as photos together.

After Your Session

Your photos will be edited for natural color, flattering light, and a polished finish, then delivered digitally within 2 to 3 days. Your download link will remain available for 60 days.
