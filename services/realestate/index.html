Before Your Session

We'll confirm the address and access beforehand, whether that's an agent, the owner, or a lockbox. Make sure to complete the prep list before I arrive to shoot, this is sent via email.

During Your Session

If there's a specific shot the listing needs, tell me before the shoot and I'll get it. Rooms should be clear of people and pets while I'm shooting, and the session moves fastest when the occupants are out.

After Your Session

Photos are edited and delivered as high-resolution JPEGs through an online gallery within 2–3 days. The link stays open for 60 days, so you have time to download what you need for the listing.

Ready to List?

Send the property address and a date that works. I'll confirm availability, and your time is held once payment is in. Reschedules and cancellations follow the standard policy.
