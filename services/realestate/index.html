What to Expect

Before Your Session

We'll confirm the address and access beforehand — agent, owner, or lockbox all work. Most of what makes a listing photo work happens before I arrive, so I'll send this list with your confirmation:

  1. Lights on — every overhead, lamp, and under-cabinet light.
  2. Blinds and curtains open, fully pulled back from the glass.
  3. Cars moved out of the driveway.
  4. Trash and recycling bins out of sight, including from the backyard.
  5. Counters clear — dishes away, sponges and soap under the sink, knives and mail in a drawer.
  6. Personal items tucked away — photos, prescriptions, calendars, anything with a name on it.
  7. Beds made, towels matched and hung, toilet lids down.
  8. Pets out of the house, bowls and beds put away.
  9. Screens off, cords tucked — TVs, monitors, chargers, ceiling fans.
  10. Outside tidy — hoses coiled, mats straight, patio furniture arranged, lawn clipped back from walkways.

During Your Session

Main living spaces and bedrooms come first, then bathrooms and utility areas. Rooms should be clear of people and pets while I'm shooting, and the session moves fastest when the occupants are out — no one being shuffled room to room.

If there's a specific shot the listing needs, tell me before the shoot and I'll get it. That includes the easy ones to forget: attic access, outbuildings, community amenities, a particular view.

After Your Session

Photos are edited and delivered as high-resolution JPEGs through an online gallery within 2–3 days. The link stays open for 60 days, so download what you need for the listing while it's live.

Ready to List?

Send the property address and a date that works. I'll confirm availability, and your time is held once payment is in. Reschedules and cancellations follow the standard policy.
